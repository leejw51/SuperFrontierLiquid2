What it is · as of 2026-10-10무엇인가 · 2026-10-10 기준

Validators that order by quorum, a checkable change log, and an order book that lives in memory정족수로 순서를 정하는 검증자들, 검증 가능한 변경 로그, 메모리에 사는 오더북

SuperFrontierLiquid is a Layer 1 exchange shaped after Hyperliquid: on-chain central limit order books for spot, perpetual and dated futures across seventeen instruments. Since ADR 0008 (2026-09-25) its blocks are ordered by a Byzantine-fault-tolerant quorum of validators and executed after commit: a chained HotStuff with two-chain finality, four validators tolerating one liar. Every validator executes the committed sequence itself and checks a hash chain over what changed; an executed block carries its proposal and quorum certificate as a finality proof. The single sequencer of ADR 0007 is still there as the other scheme, and still what make start runs. Since then: the BFT chain commits 60,000 signed orders/s at 5 ms p50 (2026-09-29); block identity excludes signatures and every derivative fill needs a mark fresher than 600 blocks (ADR 0009, 2026-09-30); and an order the executor refuses is reported at once on streamRejected instead of sitting pending (2026-10-10). SuperFrontierLiquid는 Hyperliquid의 형태를 따른 레이어 1 거래소입니다. 현물·무기한·만기 선물 17개 상품의 중앙 지정가 오더북이 온체인에 있습니다. ADR 0008(2026-09-25) 이후 블록은 비잔틴 장애 허용 검증자 정족수가 순서를 정하고, 커밋 뒤에 실행됩니다. 2체인 확정성을 가진 체인드 HotStuff이며, 검증자 넷이 거짓말쟁이 하나를 견딥니다. 모든 검증자가 커밋된 순서를 직접 실행하고 변경분의 해시 체인을 대조하며, 실행된 블록은 제안과 정족수 인증서를 확정성 증명으로 싣고 다닙니다. ADR 0007의 단일 시퀀서는 다른 방식으로 남아 있고, make start가 여전히 그것을 돌립니다. 그 뒤로: BFT 체인은 서명 주문 60,000건/s를 p50 5 ms에 커밋하고(2026-09-29), 블록 식별자는 서명을 제외하며 모든 파생상품 체결은 600블록 이내의 마크를 요구하고(ADR 0009, 2026-09-30), 실행기가 거부한 주문은 대기 상태로 남는 대신 streamRejected로 즉시 보고됩니다(2026-10-10).

It is a consensus-neutral change if the fill sequence is preserved, and the determinism test already checks that.체결마다 BTreeMap<(price, seq), H256> 조회, BTreeMap<H256, RestingOrder> 조회, 그리고 각각 삭제를 합니다. u32 슬랩 핸들 큐를 가진 가격 레벨 사다리로 바꾸면 최우선 레벨은 포인터 하나, 체결은 pop 하나가 됩니다. 체결 순서만 보존하면 합의에 중립인 변경이며, 결정성 테스트가 이미 그것을 확인합니다.

Order rows written once per block; a block journal as the durability point주문 행은 블록당 한 번 쓰기, 블록 저널을 내구성 지점으로engine

Both are on ADR 0007's list and not started. The first removes a put and a delete per mutation in favour of the book's final state. Give signature recovery its own machines서명 복구에 전용 머신을verified

Recovery already uses the C secp256k1 library, so there is no crate to swap. The gap from 36,806 to 60,166 orders/s came from taking the gateway, oracle and web off the box; it is a cores problem before it is a code problem. The design the owner named, a risk engine ahead of the sequencer, is a tier of verifiers that scales sideways and forwards VerifiedTx.복구는 이미 C secp256k1 라이브러리를 쓰므로 바꿀 크레이트가 없습니다. 36,806에서 60,166 주문/s로의 차이는 게이트웨이·오라클·웹을 머신에서 뺀 것만으로 생겼습니다. 코드 문제이기 전에 코어 문제입니다. 소유자가 말한 설계, 시퀀서 앞의 리스크 엔진은 수평으로 늘어나며 VerifiedTx를 전달하는 검증 계층입니다.

Shorter blocks, now that sealing is off the thread봉인이 스레드를 떠났으니 더 짧은 블록latency

The loaded runs cut blocks of 48,000 orders every 230 ms. Submit-to-fill is mostly waiting for the next cut. The per-block fixed cost that once argued for big blocks now runs on the writer, so the interval can come down toward Hyperliquid's ~70 ms. A trade-off to measure, not a defect.부하 실행은 230 ms마다 48,000개 주문 블록을 자릅니다. 제출→체결 시간은 대부분 다음 자르기를 기다리는 시간입니다. 큰 블록의 근거였던 블록당 고정 비용은 이제 라이터에서 돌아가므로, 간격을 Hyperliquid의 약 70 ms 쪽으로 내릴 수 있습니다. 측정할 트레이드오프이지 결함이 아닙니다.

A validator rejoining under sustained load부하가 계속되는 중에 다시 합류하는 검증자engine

BFT ordering is in (ADR 0008): rounds, votes, timeout certificates, leader reputation, execution after commit. A validator killed and restarted on a quiet chain syncs the blocks it missed and votes again within seconds. Restarted while 20,000 orders/s keep coming, it syncs and stays consistent but, on a machine it shares with three other validators and the load generator, had not resumed voting when a 75 s window ended: the tip moves faster than a quarter of one chip can re-verify. "Decentralized" now describes the ordering, with one honest caveat"탈중앙"은 이제 순서 결정을 설명하는 말입니다, 한 가지 솔직한 단서와 함께

Four validators order every block by quorum; one may lie or die and the chain continues, measured with one killed under load. Every validator verifies every signature and executes every committed payload, and audits the others' executed roots two blocks later. What the numbers leave out: all four validators ran on one machine, so the commit latencies have no network in them, and the committed rate is capped by four copies of signature recovery sharing eighteen cores rather than by the protocol. Hyperliquid's 200,000 orders/s is a many-validator figure on dedicated hardware. Recoverable ECDSA with domain separation

Consensus

Chained HotStuff over four validators (ADR 0008)

  • Rounds with a rotating leader; a quorum certificate or a timeout certificate ends each round; two consecutive certificates commit. Quorum is 3 of 4, tolerating one Byzantine validator
  • Consensus orders, execution follows: committed payloads execute on every validator; an executed block carries its proposal and certificate in parent_qc as a finality proof
  • Leader reputation benches a validator whose round timed out; a restarted validator rejoins from its executed head, by proposal requests or by block sync
  • ADR 0009 (2026-09-30): block identity is keccak256("CB-BLOCK-ID-V2\0" ‖ bincode((height, prev_hash, tx_root, state_root, timestamp, view))), signatures and certificates excluded; a refused transaction stays in a BFT block with applied false and consumes its nonce; a mark older than 600 blocks refuses trading and liquidation but not cancels
  • The sequencer scheme (make start): one proposer fixed at genesis, replicas attest with 2-of-3 votes, a follower takes over a dead leader; crash faults only
  • cb-net adds a gossip mesh with peer scoring; the engine holds no database and cannot adopt a block itself

The derivatives engine

cb-engine depends on cb-types and thiserror and nothing else: no clock, no I/O, no randomness, which is what makes replaying a block deterministic.

Margin
Notional is qty × mark / 1e10. Equity is collateral minus bad debt plus unrealised PnL; initial and maintenance requirements come from a leverage-tier table in basis points. An order is admitted against the worst-case resulting position. A resting perpetual order reserves no collateral yet, which the architecture notes as a known gap.
Funding
Premium index is an EWMA (one eighth per oracle update) plus an interest term, clamped to the market's cap. The cumulative index accrues per block. Funding is applied by an explicit FundingTick transaction that only the proposer may send and that the block builder refuses to omit: funding is consensus, not a cron job. Every chain started from the shipped genesis once halted at height 3,600 because nothing produced that tick.
Dated futures
Each future has an expiry height. Settlement uses a TWAP of marks over a final window, or the oracle price at expiry; the mark clamp tightens as expiry approaches. Settlement is a required system transaction, like the funding tick.
Liquidation and the insurance fund
A LiquidationTrigger names an owner and a market; the executor re-checks the condition, cancels the owner's resting orders on that market, then closes through the ordinary matcher. The fee is 50 bps of notional, 30 to the insurance fund and 20 to the keeper. The fund is the margin row at the protocol-pinned Address::INSURANCE, seeded from genesis; it absorbs bad debt as it is recorded, and if exhausted the market drops to reduce-only.
Oracle and mark price
Every OracleUpdate is checked against the market's oracle_authority; any other sender gets NotAuthorized. Mark is a clamped median of oracle, book mid and last trade. The authority key reaches cb-oracle as an environment variable, never on the command line.
